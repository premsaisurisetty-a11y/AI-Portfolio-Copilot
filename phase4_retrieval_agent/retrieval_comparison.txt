Phase 4 Retrieval Comparison

Question 1:
Why did this portfolio underperform last quarter?

Without Retrieval:
The answer was generic and stated that more portfolio details would usually be needed to explain underperformance. It referred broadly to market conditions and investment strategy without using actual portfolio facts.

With Retrieval:
The answer used retrieved portfolio and market context. It specifically referred to:
- weakness in midcap and international allocations
- volatility in domestic equity markets
- technology sector valuation pressure
- the portfolio’s actual structure and market backdrop

Improvement:
The response became more grounded, specific, and useful for advisory purposes.

--------------------------------------------------

Question 2:
Is this portfolio suitable for a moderate-risk client?

Without Retrieval:
The answer was general and said that portfolio suitability could not be assessed without more information about holdings, risk tolerance, and goals.

With Retrieval:
The answer used retrieved information from:
- client_profile.txt
- portfolio_notes.txt

It referenced:
- moderate risk profile
- 10-year investment horizon
- wealth creation objective
- current allocation of 70% equity, 20% debt, and 10% cash
- concentration in technology and financials

Improvement:
The response became personalized and more practical because it evaluated suitability using the available client and portfolio information.

--------------------------------------------------

Question 3:

Without retrieval, the system produced generic financial advisory responses. With retrieval, the system generated grounded, portfolio-aware, and client-aware responses. This improved explainability, practical usefulness, and safety.
